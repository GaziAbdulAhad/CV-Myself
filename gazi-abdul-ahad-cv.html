About

The best systems sit quietly in the background.

I'm a computer science graduate from Green University of Bangladesh, and over the last 6.3 years I've moved from customer-facing technical support into running NOC operations for an ISP. What I like about network work is the diagnosis — finding exactly where a link breaks, a route flaps, or a client's connection actually fails, and fixing it before it becomes everyone's problem.

Outside of routing and switching, I keep a habit of building things end to end — a hospital management system, a working 3-axis CNC machine, small web pages — mostly to stay comfortable moving between infrastructure and software. I also spent three years volunteering at a community clinic, which is where a lot of my patience for troubleshooting under pressure actually comes from.
